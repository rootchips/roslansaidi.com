FedEx Courier iLearning is an internal online training system developed for FedEx employees, specifically couriers and operations staff. It provides structured modules on company policies, delivery procedures, safety standards, and technology use. Through interactive lessons and progress tracking, the platform ensures every courier understands FedEx’s internal processes and maintains consistent service quality across all regions.<br><br>
