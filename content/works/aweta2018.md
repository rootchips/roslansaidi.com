AWETA is a national project to empower women to excel through extreme sports. Its mission is to develop five deserving women to embark on an expedition to Antarctica and retrieve the time capsule planted by our National Expeditor, Dr. Sharifah Mazlina Syed Abdul Kadir, in 2004.
As part of this initiative, I developed a digital voting management system that enables the public to nominate and vote for participating women candidates. The system ensures transparency, accuracy, and fairness through secure authentication, real-time vote tracking, and detailed analytics and helping AWETA select the most deserving expedition members through public engagement.
